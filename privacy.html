Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers "19foot.ball uploader", the internal tool of the 19 Kora YouTube channel (@19foot_ball).

Who uses it

Only the channel owner, to manage the channel's own videos. The tool has no other users and no public sign-up.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, with the owner's permission, the tool:

  • uploads the channel's own videos and sets their title, description, tags and publish time;
  • creates the channel's playlists and adds the channel's videos to them;
  • posts the channel's own comment under the channel's videos;
  • reads the channel's own video and channel information to confirm uploads.

It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube user.

Where data is kept

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than Google. The tool keeps a local list of the channel's uploaded video IDs.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token and the local list of video IDs are deleted within 7 days. Data obtained from YouTube API Services is not kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed, and can be deleted at any time on request to 19.ball.football@gmail.com.

Cookies

The tool runs on the owner's computer and does not place cookies or similar technologies on any user's browser or device.
